This Privacy Policy explains how Statusfaction – Custom status bar icons for Galaxy devices ("Statusfaction," "the App," "we," or "our") handles information when you use the App. Your privacy is fundamental to how Statusfaction is built: the App does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

1. Data Collection and Sharing
Statusfaction operates entirely offline and locally on your device.

No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, access, or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location), device identifiers, app activity, contacts, messages, photos, or diagnostics.

No Data Transmission or Distribution: Statusfaction does not transmit data to external servers, does not distribute any information to third parties, and does not inject, modify, or serve external content.

No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising: The App does not include third-party tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics, or advertising networks. (Note: Remove or update this bullet if you later integrate Google Play Billing, AdMob, or Firebase Crashlytics.)

2. Use of the AccessibilityService API
To provide its core functionality—allowing you to customize status bar icons on Samsung Galaxy devices—Statusfaction requires permission to use the Android AccessibilityService API.

Sole Purpose: The AccessibilityService permission is used strictly to render and overlay customized visual icons over your device's status bar.

What the App Does Not Do: Statusfaction cannot and does not use the AccessibilityService API to read, capture, log, store, or transmit your screen content, keystrokes, passwords, notifications, personal messages, or browsing activity. It does not alter your device settings without your action, bypass Android privacy controls, prevent you from disabling or uninstalling apps, or perform automated actions on your behalf.

Understanding Android & Google Play Protect Warnings: When you enable Accessibility permissions on your Galaxy device, Android and/or Google Play Protect display a standard system warning stating that the app may be able to observe your actions or retrieve window content. This is an automatic, generic warning shown for every application that uses the AccessibilityService API because Android groups visual overlay capabilities under the same permission framework. Despite that standard system prompt, Statusfaction contains no functionality to collect, read, or transmit your screen data.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion
Local Preferences Only: Any customization settings you choose (such as icon styles, colors, or layout preferences) are stored exclusively in local storage on your device.

Data Deletion: Because we do not collect or store any user data on external servers, there is no remote data to retain or delete. You can delete all locally saved App preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling Statusfaction.

Revoking Permissions: You can disable the AccessibilityService permission at any time through your Galaxy device's Settings – Accessibility – Installed apps menu.

4. Security
Because Statusfaction processes all status bar customization locally on your device without transmitting user data over the internet, your personal information never leaves your phone through the App.

5. Children's Privacy
Statusfaction does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

If future versions of the App introduce any new data practices or permissions, we will update this policy and notify users inside the App before those changes take effect.
